📐 Formula Bank

Important Class 12 Physics formulas for quick revision.

Coulomb: F=(1/4πɛ₀)q₁q₂/r²
Electric field: E=(1/4πɛ₀)q/r²
Potential: V=(1/4πɛ₀)q/r
Capacitance: C=Q/V
Ohm's law: V=IR
Resistance: R=ρL/A
Power: P=VI=I²R=V²/R
Magnetic force: F=qvB sinθ
Induction: ɛ=-dΦ/dt
AC reactance: Xₗ=ωL, X꜀=1/(ωC)
Mirror: 1/f=1/v+1/u
Mirror magnification: m=-v/u
Lens: 1/f=1/v-1/u
Lens magnification: m=v/u
Lens power: P=1/f (f in metre)
Snell: μ₁ sin i=μ₂ sin r
Critical angle: sin C=1/μ
YDSE: β=λD/d
Photon: E=hν
de Broglie: λ=h/p
Radioactivity: T½=0.693/λ
Transistor: I_E=I_C+I_B
